## Bounded batch

Six DC/AC/TRAN jobs over the two sources plus an independent two-point1D AC Sweep
are at most8 attempts,128MiB each,1GiB total new reservation. One of the six DC
jobs is submitted through actual Codex after its exact configured provider/grant
is observed; the same UUID is then queried, retried and observed after reconnect.
An app submission must not be replaced by a CLI or SDK-only submission.
Specification checks reuse these extracted results and add no simulator work.

For each job record purpose, exact conditions, plan/UUID, expected reservation,
latest physical counter, grant remainder and disk observation before dispatch.
A failed/unknown simulation, extraction, trust, protected-input, authority, ledger
or disk check stops new admission. Preserve the failed job and reservation; no
new-ID automatic replacement, refund, deletion, budget increase or quota reset.
Any repair-driven revalidation has a separate bounded record within the same
actual cumulative ceilings; check the existing same-change correction limit too.
